MapleSteps Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Published by Pixel Nest

In short: MapleSteps has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet. Everything a child does in the app stays on that device.

Who we are

MapleSteps is a learning app for children from Preschool to Grade 5, based on the British Columbia curriculum. It is published by Pixel Nest. You can contact us at buildpixelnest@gmail.com.

What the app stores on the device

To work, MapleSteps saves the following only on your device:

  • A child's optional nickname, chosen avatar and learning level. We ask for a nickname only, never a full name.
  • Learning progress: which questions were answered, whether they were right, streaks and badges.
  • App settings, such as sound and read-aloud choices.
  • A parent PIN, kept in the device's secure storage so it is not readable as plain text.

We do not receive, copy or have access to any of this. It never leaves the device.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is needed, and we do not ask for an email address or phone number.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics software.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or any device identifier.
  • We do not sell, share or send personal information to anyone.

Children's privacy

MapleSteps is designed for children. Because the app collects no personal information from children and sends nothing off the device, there is no personal information about a child for us to store, use or disclose. Parent-only areas (settings, deleting data, purchases) are protected by the parent PIN. If you have questions about children's privacy, please contact us at the address above.

Features that use your device

  • Read aloud. The app reads questions aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech. Depending on your device and its settings, that speech service (provided by the device maker or Google) may process the question text. The text is lesson content only, not personal information about your child.

Purchases

If the app offers a one-time purchase to unlock all topics, it is handled by Google Play. Payment details go to Google, not to us. We do not see your card or account details. The purchase can be restored on a new device through your Google Play account.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on the device until you remove it. A parent can reset a child's progress or delete all local data from the app's parent area, and uninstalling the app removes everything. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing to request from us.

Security

Learning content inside the app is encrypted, the parent PIN is protected using the device's secure storage, and the app does not send data over the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Material changes will also be explained in the app.
